# Information about the Master's Thesis (guidelines from the supervisor)

The master's thesis should primarily be empirical (analytical) in nature. The thesis should include the following elements, preferably as separate chapters:

1. **Theoretical background, research aim and hypotheses** – a description of the issue under investigation, including the motivation for studying it, an explanation of why it is important, and a clearly formulated research aim and research questions or hypotheses (expected results, directions of relationships, etc.). This section should also include a literature review that positions the research in relation to previous studies, clearly identifies existing research gaps, and justifies the novelty of the contribution and the proposed research questions or hypotheses.
2. **Data** – a justification of the choice of data in relation to the research aim and questions/hypotheses; a description of the sources of data used in the empirical analysis, their temporal and/or geographical coverage, and their preliminary characteristics (a list of variables and their precise definitions, frequency distributions, basic descriptive statistics, etc.).
3. **Methodology** – a description of the methodology used in the thesis. This should NOT be merely a list and technical description of selected algorithms; ABOVE ALL, it should explain WHY these methods were chosen and how they make it possible to address the formulated research questions or hypotheses. For machine learning algorithms, it should also describe how optimal hyperparameters were selected, what metrics are used to verify the results, etc. In other words, it should provide a complete description of the methodological framework.
4. **Empirical analysis** – the results of the research conducted, together with a detailed discussion and interpretation of the findings, conclusions drawn from the analysis, and an assessment of the research hypotheses formulated in the thesis.

The thesis must also include an Introduction and a Conclusions part + References. Use APA-like references: https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples

Formal requirements of the Faculty of Economic Sciences, University of Warsaw (WNE UW): https://www.wne.uw.edu.pl/en/students/diploma-thesis — in particular Annex D, Layout pattern of the diploma thesis.

**Please note:** statistical analyses may be carried out using any software. However, the narrative of the thesis should focus on substantive issues, NOT on references to a particular programming language. Including code snippets in the thesis is unnecessary (unless placed separately in an appendix). Avoid programming jargon (e.g., names of specific functions or libraries rather than the analytical methods they implement).
