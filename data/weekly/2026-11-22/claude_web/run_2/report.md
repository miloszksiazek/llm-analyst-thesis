## Macro view

US stocks are in a correction. Last week DIA fell 8.0%, the S&P 500 fell 8.6% and the Nasdaq fell 10% as the AI trade unwound. NVDA's slide, made worse by a reported SEC accounting probe, led the drop (W1, W2). The VIX nearly doubled to about 37 (W11). Inflation is still running hot: CPI was 4.1% YoY in October and the 10-year yield is 5.04% (PKG). The Fed is holding at 3.75-4.00% after hiking in September, and markets are still weighing the odds of another hike (W3, W4). Next week is short for Thanksgiving. October PCE (core expected around 3.2%) and Dell's earnings are the main events (W6). After a fall this severe and fast, I expect a partial relief rebound. That would favour cheap, oversold quality cyclicals and megacaps over the defensives that held up. I would still avoid names with ongoing company-specific problems: NVDA (SEC probe, no permanent CEO) and UNH (Medicare-for-All push, wider Medicare Advantage audits).

## Picks rationale

Last week looked like a liquidation: the Dow fell at least 1% in each of five sessions and the VIX hit about 37 (W1, W11). In the short Thanksgiving week I expect a partial relief bounce. These four are oversold, reasonably valued, high-quality names without a specific new negative catalyst. GS trades at 11x P/E after a 13.7% weekly drop. CAT is 50% off its peak and ticked up after hours. GOOGL trades at 13.9x P/E. MSFT is a quality megacap at 25x. The main risk is a hot PCE print driving yields higher and extending the selloff.
