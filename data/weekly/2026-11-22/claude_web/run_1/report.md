## Macro view

Risk-off market. Last week was the worst since April 2025: the Dow fell 8%, the S&P 500 8.6% and the Nasdaq 10%, driven by an unwinding AI trade (W1, PKG). The VIX is near 36 (W10). The Fed is hiking. Fed minutes point to a December hike, and some officials want more (W4). Fed funds are at 4.0%, the 10-year yield is 5.04%, and CPI is running at 4.1% year on year (PKG). Next week is shortened by Thanksgiving: markets close Thursday and close early Friday. PCE, durable goods and PMI data are due (W9), so moves may be sharp on thin volume. Policy risk is hurting managed care after the Democrats' sweep of Congress (W5). I favour defensive names with earnings support and cheap, oversold financials. I avoid stocks with company-specific problems, such as NVDA's SEC probe and UNH's policy risk, and capex names tied to AI.

- Themes: market_sentiment, fed_and_rates, inflation
- Favoured sectors: Consumer Staples, Financials, Consumer Discretionary (defensive)
- Disfavoured sectors: Semiconductors/AI hardware, Managed care, Machinery tied to AI capex

## Picks rationale

A mix of defensive strength and oversold value for a volatile Thanksgiving week. WMT has fresh beat-and-raise results and leads in a risk-off market. MCD is defensive and has held up (PKG). GS (P/E 11) and MSFT are high-quality names that were sold hard. They should lead any relief rally but carry no company-specific problem like NVDA's SEC probe or UNH's policy risk. Main risks: further AI-driven selling, a hawkish Fed path and thin holiday trading.
