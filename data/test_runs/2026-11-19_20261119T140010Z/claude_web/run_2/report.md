## Picks rationale

The Fed is turning hawkish, with hike signals and a 5.1% 10-year yield, and the AI trade looks fragile (W8, W1). So I lean toward low-beta stocks with strong earnings. WMT just beat and raised guidance. PG and KO have shown relative strength while the market fell. GOOGL adds a contrarian value angle: lowest P/E in the group after a 17% monthly fall. The risk to this mix is a broad rally in AI and cyclical stocks after Anthropic's IPO, in which these defensive names would lag.
