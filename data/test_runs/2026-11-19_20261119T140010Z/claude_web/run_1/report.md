## Macro view

The market is risk-off and worried about rates. CPI is running at 4.1% year on year, above the 4.0% Fed funds ceiling, the 2-year Treasury yields 4.8% and the 10-year yields 5.1% (PKG). Fed minutes and Governor Waller's comments point to more rate hikes (W1, W2). The S&P 500 fell below its 200-day moving average on Nov 18. The Dow dropped 1.1% that day as managed-care and software stocks sold off (W1, W14), and DIA lost 4.3% over the week (PKG). AI sentiment is shaky: Nvidia's CEO has resigned and China chip rules are tightening (W5, W6), and software stocks fell ahead of the Anthropic IPO (W11). Next week is the short Thanksgiving week, so trading will be thin. I favour defensive staples and consumer names that are holding up well and have positive earnings news (W7). I avoid crowded AI/semiconductor and software names, managed care facing new rules, and cyclical industrials that are breaking down.

- Themes: fed_and_rates, inflation, market_sentiment
- Favoured sectors: Consumer Staples, Consumer Discretionary (defensive), Insurance
- Disfavoured sectors: Semiconductors, Software, Managed Health Care, Industrials (cyclical)

## Picks rationale

This week favours defensive consumer names with relative strength. Hawkish Fed signals, the 10-year above 5%, and selloffs in AI, software and managed care are driving a risk-off market (W1, W2). WMT just beat and raised guidance (W7). PG and KO gained in a week when DIA fell 4.3%. MCD is up 11% in a month (PKG). All four have low beta and stable demand, which should hold up better if the selloff continues during the thin Thanksgiving week. They also avoid the specific blow-ups in NVDA, CRM and UNH.
