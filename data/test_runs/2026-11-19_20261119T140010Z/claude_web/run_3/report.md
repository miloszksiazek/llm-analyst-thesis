## Macro view

The market is in risk-off mode. DIA fell 4.3% last week (PKG). The S&P 500 dropped below its 200-day average as the Fed minutes pointed to more rate hikes (W1). Markets price a Fed hike in December with high probability (W5, W6). CPI is running at about 4.1% and the 10-year yield is near 5.1% (PKG). AI and software stocks are being repriced: Nvidia fell even after strong results because of a CEO transition (W2), and software stocks sold off ahead of the Anthropic IPO (W12, W4). A proposed CMS rule on Medicare Advantage audits hit managed care (W1, W4). Oil is falling on hopes of Iran peace talks (W14). Higher rates and slowing momentum favour defensive staples and steady consumer names over cyclicals, energy and housing-linked stocks. The coming week is shortened by Thanksgiving, so oversold large caps could bounce.

## Picks rationale

WMT beat Q3 expectations and raised its full-year guidance (W7, W8), and it has strong momentum, so post-earnings drift is likely. PG and MCD are low-beta, steady performers that should hold up while markets price in Fed hikes and yields stay above 5% (W5, PKG). GOOGL adds a rebound candidate: a P/E under 15 after a 17% monthly drop, with the Anthropic IPO a possible catalyst for its stake (W10, W4). The mix balances defensive names against the risk of a broad Thanksgiving-week rebound.
