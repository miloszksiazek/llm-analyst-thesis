## Macro view

Conditions are hawkish and risk-off. CPI is running at 4.1% y/y, the 10-year yield is near 5.1% and the 2-year near 4.8% (PKG). The Fed minutes pointed to more rate hikes, and Governor Waller called for aggressive action on inflation (W1, W3). The S&P 500 closed below its 200-day average on Nov 18. The Dow fell 1.1% that day, led by a selloff in managed care stocks after a regulatory proposal (W1, W6). Stocks with long-dated earnings are under pressure: AI and enterprise software are selling off ahead of a very large planned Anthropic IPO (W7, W8). Consumer staples and other defensive stocks have held up best (PKG). For the holiday-shortened week ahead, I favour low-beta defensive names with recent good news and insurers that benefit from higher yields. I disfavour rate-sensitive cyclicals, housing-linked stocks and managed care.

## Picks rationale

Fed minutes and officials signal more hikes, 10-year yields sit near 5.1%, and the S&P 500 has broken its 200-day average. I therefore favour low-beta names with company-specific support. WMT beat Q3 estimates and raised guidance after the Nov 18 close (W4, W5). PG and KO are defensive leaders with positive momentum. TRV is the cheapest stock in the universe at 10x P/E and benefits from higher yields. The main risk is a sharp rebound in oversold tech (NVDA, CRM, GOOGL), during which defensives would lag.
